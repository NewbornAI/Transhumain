# Transhumains - Corporate identity

*Visual identity for the corporations, religions and groups beyond the six faction plates.*

Written as design directions first, with palette tokens, then shot: every entry here is a rendered plate under the contract in [[Transhumains - Visual identity]] (2048×1152, text only where it is the subject, palette colours content and never chrome). The four nations plus Hazat and Skavs are already plated there.

## Distinctness rules

The whole point of a house style is that you can tell two of them apart in a thumbnail. The pairs that would collide, and the rule that separates them:

| Risk | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hazat vs Templars vs Max-Tak | Hazat is lean white ceremony; Templars is ornate dark steel with sigils; Max-Tak is black angular plate with a white sigil. Weight increases in that order. |
| Hazat vs Legion vs Templars | Hazat is lean white ceremony with cobalt; Templars is ornate dark steel with brass sigils and red cloth; Legion is matte black lamellar with red confined to one pauldron and a helmet crest, no ornament at all. Legion wins on blankness and on what stands behind it. |
| Alpha vs Union | Both are white civic futurism. Alpha is warm, high-key and gold; Union is porcelain, vermilion and jade with a cold key. Alpha never has a hard shadow. |
| Neumann vs Bakta | Both are Alpha healthcare-adjacent. Neumann is a chapel of the self (platinum, nerve blue); Bakta is soft and held (mint, cyan, rounded). |
| Jove vs Gaia | The sharpest fault line in the setting, and it is material. **Jove is rock and living metal** — mineral, cold, engineered to serve the habitat. **Gaia is grown wood and green** — vegetable, warm, adapted to the ecosystem. They look similar from orbit and mean opposite things. |
| Guild vs X-Cell | Both are money now. Guild is obsidian, gold and baize at a private auction — spectacle, ownership, night. X-Cell is the exchange floor, cream marble and brass — process, uniformity, ledger green. Guild spends it, X-Cell counts it. |
| Macrotek vs Skavs | Both work metal, and they must never be confused again. **Skavs is salvage**: mismatched plate, cable spaghetti, arc-welding sparks, amber work lamps, nothing on site is new. **Macrotek is volume**: immaculate graphite and steel, iron-orange gantries, safety-yellow markings, cold cell light, nothing on site is old. |
| Hunter vs Guild | Both sell to the same crowd. Hunter is warm daylight, toy blasters and a war on the skyline; Guild is a sealed room at night with no window that opens. |

## Render queue

All 29 proposed entries are shot: the four nations' corporate shelves, the Jove megacorp, and the orders and faiths that answer to no nation. Nothing is pending.

**Two houses still borrow more than a name.** `Voodoos` and `NetWatch` come straight from Cyberpunk 2077, and the Voodoos brief paraphrases the source's own description of the Voodoo Boys. A rename is not enough for either: they need a design pass that gives them their own expression before anything is released. Left as they are for now, deliberately.

Boards: `02-corporate-plates.jpg` (Hunter through Jove), `03-corporate-plates-2.jpg` (the four nations' shelves), `04-corporate-plates-3.jpg` (the remaining Xenos houses, the groups and faiths, and the four late additions). Every brief sits beside its plate in `Visual identity/`, and `01-corporate-sheet.png` indexes all 29 with their palette tokens.

Ship visuals stay out of scope: SenseNova does not render hulls well enough to spend plates on, so that goes to a mesh pipeline.
