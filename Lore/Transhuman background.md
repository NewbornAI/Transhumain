# Transhuman background

Transhuman is a science-fiction TTRPG set in the 24th century, in the genres of anticipation and hard science with realistic physics. Inspirations from The Expanse, Cyberpunk, Star Trek, Firefly, Altered Carbon, Transhuman Space, Eclipse Phase, Ghost in the Shell.

# History

## 22nd century

Earth is ravaged by climate change and local conflicts. Nations compete to colonize the solar system.

Devastating interplanetary war (Earth - Mars - Venus). Nations fall and rise, often under the thumbs of megacorporations.

Tech : fusion, laser, AI, genetics, cybernetics, carbon nanomaterials

## 23d century

The 4 major nations form an alliance called the Consortium, administered by the ONU.

The Lasersail Highway allows sub-light interstellar travel, although with decades long journeys spent in hibernation that few are willing to make. But there are no shortcuts or hidden pathways, only the cold reality of physics and bureaucracy. Relativistic speeds can only be reached with the energy from massive solar-powered lasers controlled by the ONU.

Tech : neural interface, fusion drive, AGI

## 24th century
Hubs such as Alpha Centauri, Sirius and Tau Ceti are multinational melting pots where the latest and fanciest products are available. They route ships and information to smaller systems, often featuring a single outpost and outdated tech.

Interplanetary transits take days but are vital to the supply chain so they are a daily occurrence. Most of it is automated, but interplanetary travelers are not uncommon. Most ships have fusion drives. Limits are heat, mass and, to a lesser degree, volume and cost.

Laser com relays allow interstellar communication at light speed. Despite this, information and goods take years to travel from system to system, stretching administration and creating a multitude of local sub-cultures.

Tech : Metallic hydrogen, Antimatter

# Background

* Most things are connected to the net and can be accessed via a neural interface. AR and VR are the norm. The net is intelligently compartmentalized into public and private servers. People can find privacy and peace of mind in their home and workplace.
* AIs called skillsofts serve as assistants and mentors in everyday life. They reduce workload and filter information to a digestible form. They range from dumb autopilot / crash course to fully sentient AI and companion.
* Genes can be edited with a simple pill, humans have multiple variants. Bodies can be enhanced beyond human with cybernetics or gene splicing. Aging can be cured, although only at body level - minds keep aging.
* Digital consciousnesses are common (although their legal states vary), whether artificially created (AGI), digitized from a human brain (ghost) or uplifted animals. They can upload themselves into clones, androids or any system linked to a digital brain.
* Fusion and solar technologies make energy abundant. Automated megafactories provide for all basic needs at virtually no cost, pushing the society into post-scarcity.
* International trade uses a digital currency called Credit.
* Electronic warfare is a crucial aspect of the corporate information war.

# Major nations

## Alpha

Open democracy. At its core, Alpha genuinely tries to create a society where anyone is free to pursue their passions in peaceful coexistence. Where everyone lives in luxury and good company, in a community where their voice matters.

Social networks are its essence. Does not use currency internally, but influence is power. The higher spheres hide a political mess of information campaigns orchestrated by influencers.

**Values** : education, ethics, art, culture, transhumanism

**Cultural roots** : Europe, Japan, Africa

**Corporations** : YouPub (medias), Neumann (cybernetics), Hardvard (education), Son-Y (Electronics), Hazat (ONU military), Bakta (healthcare)

## Ares

Multicultural empire. Each planet makes its own local government with very little supervision. Often devolves into gangs.

Above them lie the Ares and Athena super-AIs, only ensuring the terms of the ONU are respected enough to ensure coexistence with other nations.

Evolved from a military AI, Ares has access to immense power, but rarely wields it. He technically runs the Legion, an international police, although he will delegate to whoever is willing and only intervene if things are getting out of hand (usually when other nations start complaining too loudly).

Athena is a judge and advisor. She is wise and respected, but has no executive power. She is sometimes called to arbitrate legal disputes, but usually only serves as Ares’ “empathy”.

**Values** : freedom, diversity, strength.
**Cultural roots** : USA, India, Mars

**Corporations** : Legion (international police), Templars (private security), Macrotek (heavy industry), Radiance (energy, telecom), Skavs (recycling, maintenance), Voodoos (net wizards)

## Union

Socialist dictatorship. Everything is monitored and controlled by the Godfather, a super-AI designed to maximize happiness - although it is operating under strict rules and supervision. Surveillance is everywhere, but it is used to prevent crime, accidents and empty fridges. All needs are provided and all citizens cared for.

They don’t interact much with other nations, but their unparalleled information network is a crucial tool for the ONU.

**Values** : security, comfort, unity.

**Cultural roots** : China, Russia, Korea

**Corporations** : Ten-Cents (entertainment), TherMoss (heat management), NetWatch (cybersecurity), Stategrid Combine (energy), Zarya (aerospace)

## Xenos

Capitalist meritocracy. Corporations own the government. Universal basic income, but everything has a price. Constant flux of marketing wars.

They are not a single block, rather a multitude of conflicting voices competing for attention. But taken as a sum they control most of the interstellar trade, so they are difficult to ignore.

**Values** : free trade, exploration, innovation

**Cultural roots** : Venus, Ceres, South America

**Corporations** : Guild (transport syndicate), LEG GO (modular spaceships and buildings), SETI (xenobiology), Max-Tak (special forces), Hunter (contracts and bounties), X-Cell (economics)

# Notable organizations

* ONU : oversees interactions between nations.
* Buddhism : has adapted to modern values and way of life
* Followers of God : Religions based on the ancient and new testaments
* Mechanikon : Religion based on transhumanism and technology
* Shamanism
* Neo-Amish : minimum technology, forced by hostile worlds
* Gaia : Utopian anarchy preaching true freedom
* Jove : rising megacorp from the Jovian moons, now on Titan - full write-up in [[Transhumains - Jove]]

# Metahuman variants

* Posthuman : “human 2.0”, immune to disease and ageing, often optimized in other ways
* Spacer : designed to live in spaceships and 0g environment
* Cyborg : extensive cybernetic mods
* Ghost : digitized human
* Alien : uplifted animal
* Venusian : winged human adapted to Venus’ atmosphere
* Furry : spliced with animal genes
* many others, designed for a specific job or environment
* also many unique blends and splicings
