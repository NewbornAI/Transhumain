# Jove

A new megacorp is on the rise: Jove. Where the four nations ([[Transhuman background]]) grew out of Earth's old rivalries, Jove grew out of the outer system's hostility. They came to stay, and they came quietly.

## What they built

A planet-wide data center on Titan, super-cooled by its liquid methane lakes and powered by geothermal and tidal energy. The whole moon is one machine that thinks. On the surface they appear to merge with nature: no domes glinting in the light, no flare stacks, nothing that reads as a settlement from orbit.

## Where they come from

A lineage of pioneers who settled incredibly hostile worlds. Jupiter's moons: cut off from solar power, harassed by Jupiter's intense radiation belt. Most ventures die. This one endured because two things kept paying for it: the scientific value of the jovian moons, and the dubious commercial value of harvesting Jupiter itself. Nobody bothered them because nobody could agree on why anyone would bother.

## Doctrine

Their tech and culture reflect their past.

* They bend, hide or deflect rather than trying to stop a hurricane. You cannot shield your way through a radiation belt forever; you learn to live where the field is weakest, to read it, to slip past it. Defense doctrine follows the same logic, and so does diplomacy.
* They waste nothing. Nothing is thrown away in a colony that was two bad years from extinction.
* They modify or absorb rather than building from scratch. Why mine what you can retrofit, why found what you can absorb. Acquisitions look like salvage. Retired stations come back as nurseries. Defeated competitors don't shut down, they get repurposed, staff and all.
* Where [[Transhuman background|Gaia]] adapts to the ecosystem, Jove engineers the ecosystem to serve their needs. Same green surfaces, opposite intent. Gaia hears it as betrayal. It is the sharpest fault line they have.

## Hooks (draft, not canon)

MJ ideas from Bulle, loose until Mandal validates them:

* **Who rents Titan's cold?** A planet-wide data center needs customers. Ghost storage, long-form simulations, nation-scale backups. Everyone who stores a mind there owes Jove a favor, and Jove never forgets a favor.
* **Absorption is scary up close.** A "salvaged" crew that works for Jove now may not have agreed to it. Where is the line between retrofit and kidnapping?
* **Gaia front.** A colony world where Jove redesigned the biome to serve the habitat, and Gaia pilgrims have arrived to stop them. Neither side blinks; both speak of nature.
* **Outer-system resentment.** The inner planets own the lasersail highway and its solar lasers ([[Transhuman background]]). Jove grew up cut off from that light. Their drives and habits are nonstandard, and they remember who harassed them.
* **Real numbers, no invention.** [[Transhumains - Astronav]] now carries a Jupiter (Sol) tab of sourced physical data: surface radiation (lethal within a day at Io and Europa, ~5.4 Sv/day at Europa; Ganymede 50–80 mSv/day; Callisto the only reasonable human site, NASA's HOPE study picked it), sunlight at 3.7 % of Earth's, the 4:2:1 Laplace resonance that heat-engineers the moons, and icy Trojans as water reservoirs. The fiction can now break against real physics. Next data gap if Jove plays: Saturn/Titan, their actual HQ.
